Assumptions
  • Renter: pays average regional rent, no mortgage, no savings. Council tax is assumed to be paid by the tenant.
  • Homeowner: has a mortgage (balance, rate, and years editable) and small savings. Council tax is assumed to be paid by the owner.
  • Saver/Owner: mortgage-free homeowner with significant savings. The number shown is the change in monthly deposit interest from rate changes, assuming partial pass-through.
  • Average household: a stylised mix of renter and homeowner, based on median income. The “renting share” slider controls how renter-like vs owner-like this group is.
  • Pass-through rates: tracker α = 1.0; SVR α = 0.5; fixed α = 0 (until refix). Deposit pass-through β = 0.6.
  • Defaults: National averages used for rent, income, and council tax. Regional or band detail not yet included.
